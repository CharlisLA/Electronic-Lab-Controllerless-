   LED trigger

First Project without a controller,

to start off you need
Materials: 1x LED, 1x Resistor 220 Ω, 1x Jumper wire (male-to-male), 1 Power supply (e.g. the HW-131), 1x breadboard (any size).

Step 1. Disconnect the power before wiring anything.

Step 2. Insert the LED into the breadboard.
The longer leg = anode (+)
The shorter leg = cathode (−)
Connect the anode (+) of the LED to one end of the resistor.
Connect the other end of the resistor to the positive (+) power rail.
Connect the LED's cathode (−) to the negative (−) power rail. (use jumper wires if needed)
Connect the breadboard's positive and negative rails to the corresponding battery/power-supply terminals.
Turn on/connect the power.
The LED should illuminate.

(IMPORTANT) : if you want your LED to work, do not plug it in a horizontal way, the horizontal rows
across your breadboard are connected, so wire it vertical to the rows.
